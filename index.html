THE METHOD / VERSION 0.1

A map, not a verdict.

Musical subgenres do not form a tidy tree. This atlas maps editorial relationships: historical influence, offshoots, overlap, reaction and sound-world proximity. A line does not assert that one style invented another.

The map mixes internet-born microgenres with older local traditions that materially influenced them. Origins, emergence dates and even names can be disputed. Dates are approximate. We distinguish scenes, umbrella terms and historical precursors where possible, without treating them as equivalent categories.

This is the initial curated research set, not an exhaustive catalogue or final taxonomy. In particular, ballroom, Jersey club, footwork, gqom and baile funk have distinct local and cultural histories. Their inclusion should never imply they originated on online platforms.
