# YUZ working rules

YUZ is a platform owned by fasterthanreality. Its old agency project is historical context, not the product direction.

## Every product release

- Expand the verified artist catalogue and prioritise sounds with the weakest direct coverage. Do not pad classifications or invent artist/recording links to meet a count.
- Keep five featured artists as the intended concise discovery structure; a searchable full list is planned, not yet implemented.
- Record artist totals and directly covered sounds before/after in the release notes. Explain remaining coverage gaps.
- For an urgent corrective release, preserve existing verified entries and explicitly record why catalogue expansion was deferred. Never delay a necessary fix to satisfy growth.
- Keep macro entrances hidden when they have zero artist entries. Preserve their underlying data for future growth.
- Keep artist discovery concise. Extra descriptions belong behind an optional disclosure. The contact form belongs on its own page.
- Verify official identities, recording URLs, embeddable audio, and source references. Treat genre associations as editorial suggestions.
- Test the preview, run source and contact checks, use a PR, then release through the existing GitHub/Vercel project when deployment is authorized.
- Tag releases and save numbered source/history backups and recovery records in YUZ-Releases; publish source ZIP and manifest on GitHub Releases.

These are release-triggered responsibilities, not a timed background job. Do not promise catalogue growth between releases unless a separate automation is explicitly configured.
